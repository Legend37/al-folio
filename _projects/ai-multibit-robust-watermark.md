**Research Intern, University of California, Santa Barbara** · June 2026–present  
**Advisor:** Prof. Yuheng Bu · **Status:** ARR under submission

This project develops robust multi-bit text watermarking for content attribution. Embedded identifiers can be recovered after paraphrasing without access to the original text or watermark-specific training.

The evaluated system achieved **99.83% watermark-bit injection success** and **99.70% exact recovery of 32-bit identifiers** in 10,000 resampled trials under the evaluated rewriting protocol.

On natural text, identifiers were recovered from **all 20 evaluated Wikipedia and arXiv documents** after rewriting at a target code rate of 0.2. These results support reliable identifier recovery within the evaluated setting.
